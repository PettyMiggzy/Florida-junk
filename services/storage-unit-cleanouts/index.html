Storage Unit Cleanouts FAQs

Do I need to be present at the storage facility?

Usually we need access via your gate code or the facility manager. Many customers meet us briefly at the start.

Can you clear a unit that is packed floor to ceiling?

Yes. Larger units are quoted as a bigger load, sometimes with a second truck trip.

Will you leave the unit broom clean?

Yes, we sweep it out so it is ready to hand back.
