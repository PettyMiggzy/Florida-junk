Yard Waste Removal in Haines City & Central Florida

Florida yards produce a lot of debris — fallen limbs, palm fronds, overgrown brush and storm damage. Our yard waste removal service in Haines City and Central Florida hauls it away so you are not waiting on slow county pickup or hauling it yourself, especially after a storm.

What our yard waste removal service covers

  • Tree limbs, branches and brush
  • Palm fronds and bagged yard clippings
  • Old fencing, lumber and pallets
  • Storm and hurricane debris
  • Landscape rock, pavers and soil piles (by volume)
  • Garden structures, planters and swing sets

How it works and what it costs

Yard debris is priced by the space it takes in the truck. We load directly from your yard or curb. For large tree work, tell us the size of the pile and we will quote the load. We can handle one-time cleanups and recurring seasonal clears.

Yard Waste Removal FAQs

Do you remove storm debris?

Yes. After storms we help clear limbs, fencing and damaged items from yards and driveways.

Do you cut down trees?

We haul debris. We do not cut or remove standing trees, but we will take the limbs and logs once they are down.

Do I need to bag the yard waste?

Not necessarily. We can load loose piles directly.
