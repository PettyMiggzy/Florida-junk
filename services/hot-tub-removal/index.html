Hot Tub Removal in Haines City & Central Florida

A broken or unused hot tub is heavy, awkward and takes real equipment to move. Our hot tub removal service in Haines City and Central Florida includes cutting up and carrying out the spa, so your deck, patio or yard is clear without you needing to rent tools or call multiple companies.

What our hot tub removal service covers

  • Acrylic and portable hot tubs
  • In-ground and above-ground spas
  • Swim spas (call for details)
  • Hot tub covers, steps and equipment
  • Decks and platforms the tub sat on
  • Related pumps and equipment

How it works and what it costs

Have the tub drained and the power disconnected by a qualified electrician before we arrive. We then cut, carry and haul away the shell and equipment. Pricing depends on the size and access to the tub.

Hot Tub Removal FAQs

Do I need to drain the hot tub first?

Yes. Please drain it and have the power safely disconnected before our visit.

Can you remove a built-in hot tub?

Often yes. Describe the setup when you request a quote and we will advise.

Will you remove the deck underneath?

We can include deck or platform removal in the quote when needed.
