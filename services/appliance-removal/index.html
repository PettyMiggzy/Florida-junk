Appliance Removal in Haines City & Central Florida

Old refrigerators, washers, dryers and stoves are heavy, awkward and often not accepted in regular trash pickup. Junk Junkies Florida provides appliance removal in Haines City and surrounding Polk County cities, with a crew that disconnects, carries and hauls appliances away so you do not have to risk your back or your floors.

What our appliance removal service covers

  • Refrigerators and freezers
  • Washers and dryers
  • Stoves, ovens and ranges
  • Dishwashers and microwaves
  • Window and portable AC units
  • Water heaters and dehumidifiers

How it works and what it costs

Appliances are priced by the number of pieces and how much room they take in the truck. Bring an appliance outside if you like, or leave it where it sits and we will carry it out. Refrigerants and metal are handled so that appliances can be recycled where facilities accept them.

Appliance Removal FAQs

Can you take a fridge with food still inside?

Please empty it first. We can remove a refrigerator that is unplugged and cleared out.

Do you disconnect appliances?

We can handle simple disconnects for removal. Gas lines and complex plumbing should be shut off by a licensed professional before we arrive.

Are old appliances recycled?

Where recycling facilities accept them we route metal and appliances there rather than straight to a landfill.
