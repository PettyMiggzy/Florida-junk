Mattress Removal in Haines City & Central Florida

Mattresses and box springs are bulky, hard to bag and often rejected by regular trash service. Our mattress removal service in Haines City and Central Florida is a quick, low-cost way to clear old beds out of bedrooms, garages and rental properties — we carry them out and take them away in one visit.

What our mattress removal service covers

  • Twin, full, queen, king and California king mattresses
  • Box springs and foundations
  • Bed frames and headboards
  • Futons and daybeds
  • Memory foam and pillow-top toppers
  • Bunk beds and kids' beds

How it works and what it costs

Pricing is a simple per-piece charge for mattresses and box springs, and we can stack the removal with any other furniture or junk you want gone to save on the total. We wrap and carry pieces so nothing drags through your home.
