  • Couches, chairs, tables and household furniture

How it works and what it costs

Pricing is based on how much space your items take up in our truck plus the labor to carry them out, so a few items costs far less than a full load. We load from wherever the items are — inside the house, garage, driveway or curb — and sweep up when we finish. Share a photo in the quote form and we can often confirm pricing before we arrive.

Residential Junk Removal FAQs

Do I need to be home during pickup?

Not always. If the items are accessible and we have confirmed the job and price, we can load without you being present. Most customers like to point things out at the start.

Can you haul things from inside my home?

Yes. We carry items from any room, including upstairs, and protect doorways and floors as we go.

How soon can you come?

We often have same-day and next-day availability around Haines City. Submit the quote form or call and tell us how soon you need it.
