How it works and what it costs

Garage cleanouts are priced by volume. Tell us what you want to keep and we will pull only what you point to, or take the whole contents. We sweep the floor when finished. Hazardous items like paint, chemicals and fuel are handled separately — mention them upfront.

Garage Cleanouts FAQs

Can you clear only part of the garage?

Yes. Point out what stays and what goes and we will load only the items you choose.

Do you take paint and chemicals?

Hazardous liquids need special disposal. Let us know in advance and we will tell you what we can and cannot take.

How long does a garage cleanout take?

Most single and double garages take a couple of hours depending on how full they are.
